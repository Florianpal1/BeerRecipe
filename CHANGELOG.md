## [1.0] - 2026-10-27

### Added
- Recipe management: create, duplicate, favourite, scale.
- Tabbed editor: summary, grain bill, hops, yeasts, additives, process, brew log.
- Calculation engine: OG, FG, ABV, IBU (Tinseth, Rager, Garetz), colour (Morey), mash pH,
  water volumes, yeast pitching, priming sugar and cost.
- Recipe advisor suggesting quantified corrections against the target style.
- Initial catalogue of malts, hops, yeasts, additives, water profiles and BJCP 2021 styles.
- Brewing equipment management.
- Unit tests for the calculation engine, the advisor and the catalogue.
